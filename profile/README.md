### Our approach

**Start with the problem.** Understand the people, the workflow, and the outcome before choosing the tools.

**Make it maintainable.** Favor clear interfaces, focused components, useful documentation, and changes that are easy to review.

**Keep improving.** Test the important paths, learn from actual use, and refine what matters.
